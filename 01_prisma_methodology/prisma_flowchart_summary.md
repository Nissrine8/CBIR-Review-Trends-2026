# PRISMA Methodology Summary

This document details the Systematic Literature Review (SLR) protocol applied in the paper:
**"Deep Learning-Based Content-Based Image Retrieval: A Review of Trends, Multimodal Fusion, and Open Challenges"** (*Discover Artificial Intelligence*, Springer Nature).

---

## 1. Identification Phase
* **Search Databases:** IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect, ACM Digital Library, Google Scholar
* **Target Period:** Jan 2015 – Feb 2026
* **Initial Records Identified:** **N = 1,420**

---

## 2. Screening Phase
* **Duplicates Removed:** **N = 240**
* **Records Screened (Title & Abstract):** **N = 1,180**
* **Records Excluded (Irrelevant domain / Non-peer reviewed):** **N = 830**

---

## 3. Eligibility Phase
* **Full-Text Eligibility Assessment:** **N = 350**
* **Excluded (Lack of retrieval evaluation / Redundant CNN baseline):** **N = 222**

---

## 4. Included Studies
* **Synthesized in Qualitative Review:** **N = 128**
* **Standard Benchmark Quantitative Comparison:** **N = 68**
